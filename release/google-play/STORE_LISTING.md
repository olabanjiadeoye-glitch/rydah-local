## Full description
Rydah Local helps customers find and engage trusted local professionals across Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan, Warri and Port Harcourt.

Find a provider or post a service request, review quotes before work starts and keep the job journey organised inside Rydah. The platform is designed around identity checks, clear job records, arrival safety steps and supported payment flows.

### For customers
- Browse local professionals by service and area
- Post standard or urgent service requests
- Review provider quotes before work starts
- Use Arrival PIN and supported camera safety checks
- Track active and completed jobs
- Keep job and payment records in one place
- Access support, safety and dispute tools

### For service providers
- Create a professional service profile
- Complete identity and biometric verification where required
- Receive eligible customer requests
- Send quotes and manage accepted work
- Track job progress and earnings
- Manage supported billing, settlement and payout details

Rydah Local currently supports service categories including electricians, plumbers, AC technicians, generator technicians, cleaners and mechanics. Availability varies by city and depends on active verified provider supply.

## Reviewer instructions draft

Use the dedicated Rydah Play Reviewer customer account supplied in Play Console.

Recommended reviewer path:
1. Sign in.
2. Open **Find a Provider**.
3. Browse service categories and city filters.
4. Open **Post a Job** and review the job-location flow.
5. Open **My Jobs** to review job-state, quote and safety UX.
6. Open **Safety**, **Support**, **Privacy** and **Delete account** resources.

Provider biometric verification requires a real consenting person and camera/identity checks. Reviewers should not be asked to submit a real identity document merely to access the rest of the app. Keep a reviewer customer account available with seeded non-sensitive data.
